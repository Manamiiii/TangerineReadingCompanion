  https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js
- chi_sim.traineddata.gz from @tesseract.js-data/chi_sim 1.0.0
  https://github.com/naptha/tessdata
- eng.traineddata.gz from @tesseract.js-data/eng 1.0.0

The traineddata files use the packages' 4.0.0_best_int variants.

The npm wrapper packages declare MIT in their package metadata, but ship no
MIT license text. This metadata does not replace the training data's upstream
Apache-2.0 license. The data comes from Tesseract's tessdata_best, converted
to integer models and gzip-compressed by the naptha/tessdata project.
This repository has not modified the published .traineddata.gz files.
